The Heritage

Our Olfactory Legacy

Welcome to Alhamd Aeris, founded by Hammad Khalid. Born out of an obsessive passion for exquisite perfumery and rare attar formulations, our house is dedicated to bringing luxury scent experiences within standard reach.

Each formulation is crafted meticulously using high-grade concentrated oils and extracts, guaranteeing standard projection, rich sillage, and comforting longevity. Store Policies

1. Shipping & Delivery

We deliver across Pakistan. Standard delivery time is 3-5 business days. Delivery charges are standard flat rates calculated at checkout.

2. 50ML Gift Promotion

Every purchase of a 50ML bottle automatically includes 1 complimentary 3ML Pure Concentrated Attar Oil gift. This offer applies cumulatively to multiple 50ML bottles in a single order.

Due to personal care and hygiene standards, unsealed bottles cannot be returned. If you receive a damaged or incorrect bottle, please contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with order evidence for an immediate replacement.

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Direct Bank Transfer, JazzCash, and EasyPaisa payments.
